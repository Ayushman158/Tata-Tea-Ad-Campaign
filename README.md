# Choose Assam — Tata Tea Premium

A speculative campaign for Tata Tea Premium, made for *Designing Communication Ecologies* (M.Des Visual Communication, UPES, Semester 3).

After a flood leaves the headlines, the work of reopening begins. Choose Assam hands Tata Tea's own media (packs, shelves, billboards, digital) to the makers of Assam, and asks people to buy from them, not donate to them.

**Communication ecology:** cinematic film and social media → packaging → QR code → website → orders to makers, held together by one Choose Assam identity.

Open `index.html` in a browser to view the case study.

Speculative project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Tata Consumer Products.
